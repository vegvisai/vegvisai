# Funding and open books

VegvisAI is owned and run privately by Espen Brathaug, Oslo, Norway. Donations are welcome from launch. Every krone in and out is listed here.

## What donations pay for

1. Running costs: domain, hosting, email and tools
2. The trademark application for VegvisAI (NOK 3,800 for one class at Patentstyret)
3. Anything left is kept for the costs above, or for setting up the company that will run the platform. It is never used to buy influence, and a donation never gives better visibility, ranking or verification.

## Rules

- A donation is a gift. It gives no ownership, no vote, no ranking and no say over the index.
- We do not accept donations that come with conditions about content, listings or the rules for results.
- Donations are taxed as personal income for Espen Brathaug until there is a company [A: confirm with an accountant].
- When a company is set up (a sole proprietorship if required, or a limited company), this page says so, and the money left is moved there.
