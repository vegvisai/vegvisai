# Our pledge

*Draft for launch, 2026-10-02.*

VegvisAI is owned and run privately by Espen Brathaug, Oslo, Norway. Until a neutral owner takes over (a cooperative is the aim; an association or foundation can be discussed), I promise to run the platform by the principles below. This is a personal commitment made in public, not a contract. The real guarantee is that the code and the index are open: if I break the promise, you can take them and carry on without me.

## Principles that apply from launch

1. **Purpose.** Make it possible for every business and organisation to be read and understood by AI agents, without a middleman taking control of the road to the customer.
2. **Open to all.** All businesses, industries, organisations and AI agents, including local models, get the same access on the same terms.
3. **Nothing can be bought.** Ranking, placement, verification and access are never paid for. How the guide chooses and orders results is published.
4. **We ask only for what you publish.** The platform never asks for a business's raw data. It indexes only what the business or body itself publishes.
5. **Open licences.** The code is Apache 2.0 and the index ODbL. The licences will not be tightened.
6. **Not greedy.** Payment is for work, hosting, measurement, training and certification, never for placement or influence.
9. **Always movable.** Domains, keys and accounts are kept so they can be handed over to the next owner.
10. **Open books.** The rules for choosing results, the changes to them, and removed listings are public. Accounts are published once there is a company.

## Principles for the neutral owner

These cannot apply while one person owns the platform. They are the aim, and they will be written into the articles of the neutral owner.

7. **No dominant owner.** No single owner can gain controlling influence over the platform.
8. **A locked core.** Changing the purpose or principles 2 to 7, selling the brand, or winding up the platform requires a qualified majority of the owners, and may require approval from an independent guardian.

## How the pledge works

- **Only stricter, never weaker.** I will tighten the principles as we learn, and I will not loosen them or add rules that break them.
- **In the open.** Every change to this file is a public commit with a date and a reason.
- **Carried forward.** The principles go into the articles of association or shareholders' agreement of every owner that follows: first a limited company (AS), then the neutral owner.
- **Your remedy.** If I break it, the code and the index are yours to take under their licences. Fork us, under your own name (see [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md)).
