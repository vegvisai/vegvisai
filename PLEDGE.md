# Our pledge

*Draft for launch, 2026-10-02. Becomes binding on the day VegvisAI launches.*

Until a neutral owner takes over (a cooperative is the aim; an association or foundation can be discussed), the founders of VegvisAI pledge to follow these principles in everything the platform does.

## The principles

1. **Purpose.** Make it possible for every business and organisation to be found and understood by every AI agent, without a middleman taking control of the road to the customer.
2. **Open to all.** All businesses, industries, organisations and AI agents, including local models, get the same access on the same terms.
3. **Nothing can be bought.** Ranking, placement, verification and access are never paid for. How the guide chooses and orders results is published.
4. **Data stays with its owner.** Raw data never leaves the business. The platform indexes only what the business or body itself publishes.
5. **Open licences.** The code is Apache 2.0 and the index ODbL. The licences can never be tightened.
6. **Not greedy.** Payment is for work, hosting, measurement, training and certification, never for placement or influence.
7. **No dominant owner.** No single owner can gain controlling influence over the platform.
8. **A locked core.** Changing the purpose or principles 2 to 7, selling the brand, or winding up the platform requires a qualified majority of the owners, and may require approval from an independent guardian.
9. **Always movable.** Domains, keys and accounts are kept so they can be handed over to the next owner.
10. **Open books.** Articles, accounts, the rules for choosing results, and removed listings are public and can be checked.

## How the pledge works

- **Only stricter, never weaker.** The principles can be tightened as we learn. They can never be loosened, and no new rule, policy or feature may break them. A change that weakens a principle has no effect.
- **In the open.** Every change to this file is a public commit with a date and a reason.
- **Carried forward.** The pledge goes into the articles of association or shareholders' agreement of every owner that follows: first a limited company (AS), then the neutral owner.
- **Your remedy.** If we break it, the code and the index are yours to take under their licences. Fork us, under your own name (see [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md)).
