Vegvis · /ˈʋeːɡˌʋiːs/ or “VAYG-vees” · is Norwegian. Veg: road. Vis: show. VegvisAI shows AI the crossroad to YOUR business.

Make an AI-readable business card

Answer the questions and you get a snippet that makes your business understandable for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and local language models. The snippet is enough to register, and it makes it easier for AI to understand who you are. If you want to do more, you also get a business card page and two optional files. You put everything on your own website. You own it, and we keep no copy.

The open index

Under the open licences ODbL (the index) and DbCL (your entry), so other open guides can use it too. The choice is written into ai-catalog.json on your own website; only you can give or remove it. Copies others have already downloaded cannot be recalled. Without it you are still found in the VegvisAI guide, but not in the open export.

AI agents (experimental)

Needs the request page above. The visitor's own AI agent fills in the form and the visitor clicks Send, which opens your request page with the details in the address. Ask only for what you need: the details travel in the web address, so never ask for health information, national identity numbers or card details. WebMCP is an experiment in Chrome and can change; see the guide. Browsers that do not know it ignore the form attributes.
