Vegvis · /ˈʋeːɡˌʋiːs/ or “VAYG-vees” · is Norwegian. Veg: road. Vis: show. VegvisAI shows AI the crossroad to YOUR business.

Register your business

Does your business have an AI business card on its own website? Then it can be registered in the open guide, so AI assistants that do not know you yet can find their way to your card. It is free, and placement cannot be bought.

No business card yet? Make one here in about fifteen minutes, and run the AI check on your website first.

What happens

  1. We read /.well-known/ai-catalog.json and the business card on your own domain.
  2. Norwegian businesses: we check the organisation number in Enhetsregisteret, without bankruptcy or winding-up. EU businesses: we check the VAT number in your card (schema.org vatID) in the EU register VIES. UK businesses: the company number is checked in Companies House. Other countries: only the domain is checked.
  3. The website in the register or in your card must point to the same domain. If not, a person looks at it.
  4. We look for hidden instructions to AI in the card.
  5. At the start, a person reviews every new entry before it is shown. You can check the status below.

We do not ask for your email and keep no personal data from the form. Every listing, change and removal is in the public changelog. We re-check entries every week; if the card is gone, the entry is removed. Read how the guide chooses results.
