# Writing about VegvisAI?

Everything you need in one place: what it is, facts with sources, logo files and screenshots. Use them freely in articles about VegvisAI.

## In short

VegvisAI is an open guide that helps AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and local models find and understand businesses, public services and organisations. A business publishes a small AI business card on its own website; the guide indexes only what the business publishes, shows results in random order, and never sells ranking, placement or verification. The first step for anyone is a free AI check that shows how well an AI can read their website.

- **No ranking.** Equal terms for all.
- **Your data.** You publish it. We only index it.
- **Every agent.** Big, small, or on your laptop.

## The name

Vegvis (VAYG-vees) is Norwegian. *Veg*: road. *Vis*: show. VegvisAI shows AI the road to your business. Write it as one word with a capital V and AI: **VegvisAI**.
