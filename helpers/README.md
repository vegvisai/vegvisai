# Helpers

Developers, web agencies and consultants who help businesses become readable for AI: make the AI business card, put it on the website, fix what the AI check finds, and register in the guide. Helpers may charge for their work.

Businesses ask for help with the issue form [Find help](../../issues/new?template=find-help.yml). Anyone can answer there; being on this list is not required.

## The rules

1. **VegvisAI takes no cut, handles no payment and guarantees no work.** Every deal is between the business and the helper. Helpers are responsible for their own claims, contracts, prices, tax and VAT.
2. **Not a certification.** We check two simple criteria once (below). Check references and agree terms in writing.
3. **No ranking.** The list is sorted alphabetically by name; the order means nothing. Nobody can buy a place on it, and a business gets no advantage in the guide from using a helper: the same rules apply whether it does the work itself or pays someone.
4. **No exclusivity.** Anyone who meets the criteria can join, with no limit per country. Nobody gets priority, sole rights or customers assigned.
5. **Be honest.** Never promise «better ranking» or to be found by every AI. Say «readable for AI assistants»; we cannot guarantee that a given AI service reads the files.
6. **Data stays with the business.** The files go on the business's own domain. Ask for your own login with limited access, never for passwords, and give the access back when the job is done. Keep no copy beyond the job; where you process personal data for the business, agree it in writing.
7. **Do not agree prices** with other helpers.
8. **Removal.** A helper who breaks these rules is removed. The pull request names only the rule number; the reasons are given to the helper privately, and the helper may answer first.

Report fraud, unlawful content or broken rules privately to [contact address], not in a public issue.

## Join the list

Show that you can do the job, then add yourself to `helpers.json` in a pull request, in alphabetical order by name:

1. **One AI business card that scores 100** in the AI check, on your own domain, or for a business that confirms in the pull request that it may be named.
2. **One merged pull request** in this repository (a template, a check, index data, a translation, a guide).

```json
{
  "name": "Your name or company",
  "github": "your-github-username",
  "countries": ["NO"],
  "languages": ["nb", "en"],
  "website": "https://your-site.example",
  "proof_card": "https://the-card-that-scores-100.example/",
  "proof_pr": 123,
  "offers": "One sentence: what you help with, and whether you charge"
}
```

Your entry is public on GitHub and stays in the git history. No phone numbers or private addresses; link to your own website for contact. See the [privacy notice](https://vegvis.gixer77.com/privacy/).

## Ambassadors

One ambassador per country helps «The first 100» there. Ambassadors are volunteers. They get their name and country on the website, and may earn from helping businesses like every helper. **The role gives no right or priority to tasks, employment or payment, now or later.** You choose what you do and can stop at any time. Ambassadors are listed as helpers only when they meet the same criteria as everyone else.

A small fee from donations may come later, only after the tax rules are checked and with a written agreement that says who pays; it is never promised in advance.
