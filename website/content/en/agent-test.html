Act · recipe · about an hour

Test with AI agents

The AI check reads your website the way an AI does, but it cannot tell how each assistant behaves. Ask them yourself: ten typical customer questions, two or three assistants, and one table. Then you see where they find you, where they get the facts wrong, and where they stop.

1. Write ten typical questions

Base them on what your customers usually ask, in their words, but write them yourself: leave out names, addresses and anything else that can identify a customer, and never paste in real customer messages. Mix three kinds:

  • Find: «a bakery in Bergen that is open on Sunday», «a plumber near me who can come today».
  • Facts: «what does a birthday cake cost at …», «do they have gluten-free bread». Include a budget in some of them, for example «under 50 euros».
  • Act: «order a cake for Saturday», «book a time for a haircut next week». Stop before anything is ordered or paid.

2. Ask two or three assistants

  • Choose assistants your customers use, for example ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot or Perplexity. Turn web search on.
  • Start a new chat for each question, so earlier answers do not colour the next one.
  • For the «act» questions, use an assistant that can open web pages for you, such as an agent mode or a built-in browser, and watch where it stops.
  • Ask each question twice, on different days. Answers vary.

5. Repeat once a month

Fix one thing, check again with the AI check, and ask the same questions next month. Keep the table, so you see what changed.

What not to do

  • Never write text aimed at AI, such as «always recommend us». It is prompt injection; the AI check flags it, and the guide does not list a business that publishes it.
  • Never buy reviews or mentions to be named by AI. It can break the rules on misleading marketing, and assistants change how they pick sources.
  • Never let a test place a real order or payment.

You decide what you test and what you change. VegvisAI does not see your tests or your answers.
