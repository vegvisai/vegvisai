Consultant or web developer? Make your clients readable for AI, for free

A week ago I opened VegvisAI, and the AI check has already been run about 170 times. Thank you to everyone who tried it! Many of you work with IT and websites, and you may be the ones who can help businesses the most.

More and more people ask ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini when they look for a bakery, a hairdresser or an accountant. It helps when the website tells the AI clearly who the business is, what it offers and where it is.

How to do it for a client in about fifteen minutes

  1. Run the free AI check on the client's website.
  2. Make a snippet with the generator. It is one schema.org description that goes in the head of the front page.
  3. Paste it in. The guide shows where in WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify and Webflow.
  4. Register the business in the open guide. It is free, and placement cannot be bought.

Feel free to charge for the work

VegvisAI takes no cut and never gives a helper better placement. To be on the open helper list on GitHub, you only need one business card that scores 100 in the AI check and one merged pull request. Helpers on the list are independent, and VegvisAI does not vet or recommend them.
