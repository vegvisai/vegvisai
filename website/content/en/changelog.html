Changelog

What's new

Every change to the guide, the AI check, the generator and the open index, newest first. The code and its full history are open on GitHub. Changes to the register itself (businesses added or removed) are in the public register changelog.

21 October 2026

  • Blog: Open to AI agents doesn't mean open to everything, on what an ordinary business can do when big shops close or open up to agents.
  • AI check, bot protection: when the website answers with what looks like bot protection or a block on automated visits, the check says so instead of only «could not be measured», and names the provider when it can tell (for example Cloudflare). The same protection can stop AI assistants that fetch the page when someone asks, even when robots.txt lets them in. Our check always says openly who it is; it never pretends to be another bot.
  • AI check, possible ways for a customer's agent: for businesses, the report shows the signs it finds that an agent can learn about the business, ask it (email, phone, form or contact page) and book or order (booking or shop platforms, schema.org actions, WebMCP tools, booking or order links). It does not test that they work with a given assistant, it is information only and does not change the score. A site with no way to reach the business gets advice to add one.
  • New guide: test with AI agents. Ten typical customer questions, two or three assistants, and a table of where they find you and where they stop, with the usual causes and fixes.
  • New guide: rules for AI agents. A template for saying what agents may do on your website: read, prepare, and nothing binding without the customer, and how to back it up.
  • Agent checkout: the guide now covers what to do when something goes wrong: wrong quantity, a retry that doubles the cart, sold out or a new price, old carts and two orders from one click.

20 October 2026

  • AI check, platforms: the report now names ready-made booking, shop and website platforms it finds on the site, such as Calendly, Shopify or WordPress. It is information only and does not change the score. Each month we count how many checks find each platform, without the address, to learn which platforms are worth a guide or a connection.
  • Businesses by place and service: the business pages now have a page per place, per service and per service in a place, for example /businesses/service/bakery/bergen/, because AI assistants usually ask for «a service in a place». The groups come from what the businesses gave themselves, the order inside is still random, and the lists of places and services are alphabetical.
  • AI check, price level: a business site with no prices or price level on the checked pages now gets advice to show prices, «from» prices or an hourly rate, or to add priceRange. AI assistants are often asked with a budget. It does not change the score. Prices in euros, pounds, dollars and Swedish and Danish kronor are now recognised as well as Norwegian kroner, so sites with such prices as plain text get the same advice about structured prices as Norwegian sites, which can lower the score.
  • Privacy page: new rows for the generator (nothing you fill in or paste is sent to us) and for email to contact@vegvis.ai.
  • Generator, only a Facebook page: the generator can fill in the form from text you copy from the business's own page, for example the «About» section on Facebook. The text is read only in your browser; nothing is fetched from Facebook and nothing is sent to us. Profiles on Facebook, Instagram and similar platforms are linked from the card (schema.org sameAs), but never used as the website, since the card has to live on your own domain. At the start we help a few businesses for free to publish the card on a domain they buy and own themselves; write to contact@vegvis.ai.

19 October 2026

  • Guides in five steps: the guides and the front page now follow Discover, Understand, Connect, Act and Control, from being found to being used by AI on your own terms, instead of levels 0 to 3.

17 October 2026

  • Clearer wording: «free» now says what is free (getting listed and the toolkit; paid help is optional), VegvisAI «never handles the money» and «never takes over the sale», and a person reviews new entries «at the start».
  • Registering is clearer: the registration page starts with three steps (check, make the card, register), checks your website at the top and fills in the form when it is ready. The details about what happens are folded away.
  • Counting the funnel without personal data: the AI check and the registration page send only the name of a step, such as «ready to register», never the web address. Nothing is stored; the count comes from aggregated traffic statistics.

14 October 2026

  • Open for AI agents: each business in the guide now shows what its own website offers AI agents: booking or order links, WebMCP tools and MCP, A2A or OpenAPI services. It comes from the business's own files, is shown in the connector, the open index and the business pages, and never changes who is shown or the order. VegvisAI charges nothing for it.
  • WebMCP, errors caught: the request form from the generator and the code in the WebMCP and agent checkout guides now catch a failed registerTool() (a taken name, or a page that has switched WebMCP off) and log a short warning instead of an uncaught error.
  • WebMCP guides, clearer about annotations: consequentialHint and untrustedContentHint are signals agents may follow, not guarantees. An order or booking still needs the customer's own confirmation, and a tool must never act on text others wrote.
  • Fixed: two links on Get started and Support now go to the English registration page.

13 October 2026

  • Generator: the WebMCP request form now works with ChatGPT. The form gets a tool registered in JavaScript, which the built-in browser in the ChatGPT desktop app uses. The tool only fills in the form, and the visitor still clicks Send. The old form attributes are gone, since ChatGPT never read them and the draft standard dropped them on 8 October.
  • WebMCP guides rewritten: the WebMCP guide and agent checkout now lead with JavaScript tools, show who supports WebMCP today (checked 13 October), and use the draft's annotations: readOnlyHint, untrustedContentHint and consequentialHint. New safety advice on iframes and on switching tools off with Permissions-Policy.
  • AI check: reads every description in a registered WebMCP tool, parameters included, and handles names and descriptions with apostrophes.

11 October 2026

  • New blog: thoughts, news and recipes. First post: how consultants and web agencies make their clients readable for AI, for free.
  • Link previews: every page now shows a title, description and image when shared on LinkedIn and elsewhere.
  • Public services: Altinn is now 11 entries by topic, from choosing a legal form to support schemes and employer duties, so an AI can send people straight to the right page.
  • AI check: a site whose front page redirects to another address is now checked where it lands.
  • Lookup counts: we count, per business and month, how often it is found through the connector and how often its page in the guide is read by AI bots and others. Only numbers, never who asked, and never shown in public. They will go to the business itself when email alerts are switched on.
  • Connector and business pages: generic types such as «LocalBusiness» are no longer shown as offers, and offer names with commas stay whole.
  • Privacy: the policy now states the basis for Cloudflare's transfers outside the EEA.

10 October 2026

  • Generator: a link to your page in the guide. llms.txt and ai-catalog.json get a link to the business's own page in the open guide, which shows whether it is registered and checked. Not in the business card itself, and you can leave it out.

9 October 2026

  • New: businesses in the guide. A page with every business that has registered, in random order and never ranked, and one page per business that links to its own website. AI assistants that search the web can now find and cite them.
  • Sitemap: now covers all the guides as well as the tools and the business pages.
  • vegvis.ai itself is AI-readable by its own yardstick: structured data on every page (who runs the site, what each page is and when it changed), the three open indexes described as datasets, a contact page, and an up-to-date ai-catalog.json.
  • AI check: fewer false alarms. Ordinary sentences such as «send the customer to the right page» are no longer flagged as attempts to leak data.

7 October 2026

  • Registration is easier. One schema.org snippet in the head of your front page is enough. llms.txt and ai-catalog.json are optional extras.
  • New guide: add the snippet to your website. Where to paste it in WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify and Webflow, and which plans allow it. Read it.
  • Generator: gives a paste-ready snippet first, and says for each file whether you need it.
  • AI check: when your site already describes the business, it says «Looks ready to register» and opens the form with the address filled in.
  • Norwegian businesses: the organisation number can be typed in the form instead of placed in the card, when Enhetsregisteret lists the same website.
  • Website: the English pages are written for businesses in any country, and of every size.

6 October 2026

  • Connector and MCP registry (1.5.1): described as what it is: a guide to businesses in any country, plus public services and political parties in Norway.
  • Connector: all five tools now declare that they only read, never delete and give the same answer each time (destructiveHint, idempotentHint), so agent directories can trust them. Found by an independent MCP trust index.
  • AI check: finds a business's own AI agent through its A2A agent card (/.well-known/agent-card.json), lists the agent's skills under «open for AI», and checks the card for hidden instructions to AI. A first step towards the customer's agent talking to the business's agent.

5 October 2026

  • AI check: a clear next step. When a site lacks a schema.org business description, ai-catalog.json or llms.txt, the result shows a button straight to the free generator with the web address filled in.
  • New guide: agents fill the cart, customers pay as today. A recipe for letting a visitor's own AI agent search products and fill the cart on the shop's website, while the customer pays in the checkout the shop already has. Read it.
  • New overview: paying through AI agents. The payment solutions and open protocols that are coming, with status and sources, listed neutrally. Read it.
  • New guide: let AI agents use your forms (WebMCP). Three HTML attributes on the forms a business already has. Read it.
  • Generator: an optional request form that AI agents can fill in, made from the business's own request details. Off by default; the customer still clicks Send.
  • AI check: lists WebMCP tools under «open for AI» (no points yet, since WebMCP is experimental) and checks their descriptions for hidden instructions to AI.
  • Registration: a business card with hidden instructions in its WebMCP descriptions is not listed.

4 October 2026: VegvisAI is open

  • The website, the AI check, the generator, registration and the connector opened on vegvis.ai, and the code repository became public.
  • The connector is listed in the official MCP registry as ai.vegvis/vegvisai.
  • AI check: main headings now give points in proportion to the pages that have one, and the advice names the pages without one. This came from the first user's feedback on launch day.
  • llms.txt and ai-catalog.json for vegvis.ai rewritten for the open platform.

3 and 4 October 2026: system test before opening

  • Finding businesses: a request now matches word by word and other word forms, so «bakeri» finds a bakery and «bursdagskaker» finds birthday cakes.
  • Registration: specific business types such as bakeries are recognised without an organisation number, and the public changelog shows the real reason for a manual review.
  • Open index: a replaced monthly release can no longer list a business that asked to be deleted.
  • Privacy: the content of removed entries is deleted after the next monthly release, at the latest after 35 days.
