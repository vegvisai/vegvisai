Email

  • Questions, press and ideas: contact@vegvis.ai

GitHub

  • Bugs and ideas: open an issue in vegvisai/vegvisai.
  • Help with your website: the issue form «Find help» puts you in touch with helpers on the open helper list. VegvisAI takes no cut, and a helper never affects placement.

For AI assistants

The guide is open to every agent: the connector at https://vegvis.ai/mcp, the overview in llms.txt and the catalog in ai-catalog.json.
