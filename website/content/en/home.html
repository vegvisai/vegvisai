Step one · free and open source

How well can an AI read your website? Find out in 15 seconds.

The AI check reads your site the way ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and local models do, and tells you what they see, what they miss, and what to fix first. It works for businesses, public bodies, NGOs and political parties, each measured by a yardstick that fits.

What it measures

  • Can AI bots get in, and find your pages (robots.txt, sitemap)?
  • Can they read the text without running JavaScript?
  • Do they understand who you are and what you offer (schema.org)?
  • Do they find the key pages: services, prices, contact, programme?
  • Is anything hidden that tries to steer an AI (prompt injection)?

What you get

  • A score out of 100, with the points for every check, so you can see why
  • A short list of fixes, most important first
  • What your site already has open for AI: feeds, search, data, APIs, MCP
  • Your site measured against a published yardstick, never ranked against others

Questions

Short answers

What does it cost?

Getting found costs nothing, and you can do every level yourself with the open toolkit. Paid help is optional: setup, hosting, monitoring, courses and certification. Ranking, verification and access are never paid.

Who owns the data?

You do. Your files live on your own website. The guide only indexes what you publish, and the index itself is open under ODbL.

What if VegvisAI is bought?

The code is Apache 2.0 and the index is ODbL, so anyone can run it. The platform is meant to move to a neutral owner with locked principles. If we ever become a gatekeeper, fork us.

Do my customers need to install anything?

No. They ask the AI they already use.
