Vegvis · /ˈʋeːɡˌʋiːs/ or “VAYG-vees” · is Norwegian. Veg: road. Vis: show. VegvisAI shows AI the crossroad to YOUR business.

Contact

Contact

VegvisAI is run by Espen Brathaug in Oslo, Norway. Write in Norwegian or English.

Email

  • Questions, press and ideas: contact@vegvis.ai
  • Remove or delete a business from the guide: contact@vegvis.ai, with the web address. See privacy for what happens next.
  • Security issues: security@vegvis.ai. Please do not report them in public issues.

GitHub

  • Bugs and ideas: open an issue in vegvisai/vegvisai.
  • Help with your website: the issue form «Find help» puts you in touch with helpers on the open helper list. VegvisAI takes no cut, and a helper never affects placement.

For AI assistants

The guide is open to every agent: the connector at https://vegvis.ai/mcp, the overview in llms.txt and the catalog in ai-catalog.json.
