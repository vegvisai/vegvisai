Vegvis · /ˈʋeːɡˌʋiːs/ or “VAYG-vees” · is Norwegian. Veg: road. Vis: show. VegvisAI shows AI the crossroad to YOUR business.

Support

Help keep the road open

VegvisAI is free, open source and run in spare time. Here is how you can help, with money or without.

Without money

  • Run the AI check on your own website, and on your municipality's or your club's.
  • Make an AI business card and register your business.
  • Join the dugnad on GitHub: code, checks, index data, translations.
  • Tell others. The guide gets better with every business and every contributor.

With money

Donations go first to running costs (domain, hosting, email and tools), and then possibly to the trademark application for the name. Every krone in and out is listed in the open ledger in FUNDING.md on GitHub.

What a donation does not buy

A donation is a gift. It gives no ownership, no vote, no ranking, no placement and no verification, and we do not accept donations that come with conditions about content or listings. See how the guide chooses results and our pledge.

Until there is a company, donations are received by Espen Brathaug privately and taxed as his income.

VegvisAI™ is run by Espen Brathaug, Oslo, Norway.
